Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026. This is a translation; the German version is legally binding.

In short

WIP does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Everything the app stores stays on your iPhone. Only if you play online does Apple (Game Center or iCloud) share your player name and your results with the other players.

2. Data in the app

WIP stores your game progress only locally on your device: countries you have mastered, records, your streak, daily challenge results and settings such as vibration. This data does not leave your iPhone and is not transmitted to us or to third parties. The app does not connect to servers of its own for its games.

For the map in the capitals game, WIP uses Apple’s map service (MapKit). For technical reasons, data such as your IP address may be transmitted to Apple to load map images. We do not receive this data. More information in Apple’s privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

You can delete all stored data at any time: Profile → Settings → “Reset progress”, or by deleting the app.

2a. Online duels with Game Center

For online duels (for example “Country Cards online”), WIP uses Apple’s Game Center. You sign in with Apple using your Apple Account; we never receive your credentials. During a duel, Apple shares your Game Center name, a player ID assigned by Apple and the state of the duel (for example cards played and points) with the other players. The game state is stored on Apple’s servers, not with us; we do not run a server of our own for this. The legal basis is providing the game you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Game Center is optional: without signing in, you can use all other games without restriction. More information in Apple’s privacy policy and under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone.

2b. Flag Challenges via iCloud

For Flag Challenges, WIP stores the challenge and its results in the app’s public iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). Stored are: the challenge code, the start time, the name you show in the challenge (your Game Center name or a name you choose), your score and playing time, and an identifier for your iCloud account assigned by Apple that is only valid for WIP. Anyone who knows a challenge’s code can see the names and results of that challenge. We can also see this data but use it only for the challenges. The data is stored with Apple; we do not run a server of our own. The legal basis is providing the game you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If you would like your results deleted, send us an email. Reminders about running challenges are local notifications on your iPhone, which you can turn off in Settings.

3. App Store and TestFlight

WIP is distributed through Apple’s App Store. When you download it, Apple processes data such as your Apple Account and purchase information under its own responsibility. If you have agreed to it in your iPhone settings, Apple provides us with anonymous statistics and crash reports. We cannot identify you personally from them.

If you take part in a test via TestFlight, Apple provides us with your name and email address (if you joined through an email invitation), as well as feedback and crash reports you send via TestFlight. We use this data only to run the test and improve the app (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

4. Contact by email

If you write to us by email, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed for this purpose.

5. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page and protect it against abuse. More information: GitHub’s privacy statement. This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party content; the fonts are hosted locally.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). To exercise them, contact the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

7. Children

WIP is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from children either.
