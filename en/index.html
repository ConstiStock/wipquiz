FAQ

How does the streak work?

Every day on which you start at least one game counts. If you skip a day, your streak starts again at 1. You can see your record in your profile.

What is the daily challenge?

Every day there are 10 questions about the flags and capitals of a different region. A new one is waiting at midnight.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. WIP has no account and does not send any data to us or to third parties. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I delete my progress?

Yes: Profile → Settings → “Reset progress”. Deleting the app also removes all data.
